🏞️ Environment

Sets the sky, light, ground, plants, rock colour and horizon. The trails themselves don't move.

📐 Scale

Map size 1.00× is true scale — a 953 m trail sign means 953 real metres. Shrink it for a quicker walk; your pup stays the same size, so the world gets smaller around it. Hill exaggeration only stretches height.

🚩 Starting point

Every dead-end on the trail network is a trailhead. Reaching any of them after 20 m of walking ends the hike.

🗺️ Trail world

Garden of the Gods loads by default. To explore somewhere else, drop in a .geojson of paths and another of areas of interest — select both at once. Add a pup-world/1 bundle from tools/fetch_dem.py for real terrain, or pass ?world=path/to/world.json in the URL. Without a bundle the map is playable but flat.
